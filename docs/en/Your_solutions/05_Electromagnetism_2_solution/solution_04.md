# 4. Magnetic Torque

Given:

$$
l = 10\,\mathrm{cm} = 0.10\,\mathrm{m}
$$

$$
w = 5\,\mathrm{cm} = 0.05\,\mathrm{m}
$$

$$
I = 2\,\mathrm{A}
$$

$$
B = 0.3\,\mathrm{T}
$$

Area of the loop:

$$
A = lw
$$

$$
A = (0.10)(0.05)
$$

$$
A = 0.005\,\mathrm{m^2}
$$

Magnetic torque:

$$
\tau = IAB\sin\theta
$$

The magnetic field is parallel to the plane of the loop. Therefore, it is perpendicular to the area vector.

$$
\theta = 90^\circ
$$

$$
\tau = (2)(0.005)(0.3)\sin 90^\circ
$$

$$
\tau = 0.003\,\mathrm{N\,m}
$$

**Answer:**

$$
\boxed{\tau = 3.0\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{N\,m}}
$$
