# 1. Gauss's Law

Given:

$$
q = +2\,\mathrm{C}
$$

By Gauss's law:

$$
\Phi_E = \frac{q}{\varepsilon_0}
$$

$$
\Phi_E = \frac{2}{8.85\times 10^{-12}}
$$

$$
\Phi_E \approx 2.26\times 10^{11}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}
$$

**Answer:**

$$
\boxed{\Phi_E \approx 2.26\times 10^{11}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/C}}
$$

The radius does not affect the total electric flux because the charge is fully enclosed by the spherical surface.
