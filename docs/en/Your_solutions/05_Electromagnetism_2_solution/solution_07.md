# 7. Wavelength and Frequency

Given:

$$
\lambda = 550\,\mathrm{nm}
$$

Convert to meters:

$$
\lambda = 550\times10^{-9}\,\mathrm{m}
$$

$$
\lambda = 5.50\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}
$$

Use:

$$
c = f\lambda
$$

$$
f = \frac{c}{\lambda}
$$

$$
f = \frac{3.00\times10^8}{5.50\times10^{-7}}
$$

$$
f = 5.45\times10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}
$$

Light with wavelength about $550\,\mathrm{nm}$ corresponds to green light.

**Answer:**

$$
\boxed{f = 5.45\times10^{14}\,\mathrm{Hz}}
$$

$$
\boxed{\text{Color: green}}
$$
