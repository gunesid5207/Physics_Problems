# 3. Biot-Savart Law

Given:

$$
I = 3\,\mathrm{A}
$$

$$
\Delta l = 0.1\,\mathrm{m}
$$

$$
r = 0.2\,\mathrm{m}
$$

The wire segment is perpendicular to the line connecting it to point $P$, so:

$$
\sin\theta = 1
$$

For a small current segment:

$$
B = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{I\Delta l\sin\theta}{r^2}
$$

$$
B = 10^{-7}\frac{(3)(0.1)(1)}{(0.2)^2}
$$

$$
B = 7.5\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T}
$$

**Answer:**

$$
\boxed{B = 7.5\times10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T}}
$$

The direction is perpendicular to the plane formed by the current segment and point $P$, using the right-hand rule.
