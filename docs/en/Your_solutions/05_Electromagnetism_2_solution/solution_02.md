# 2. Ampere's Law

Given:

$$
d = 10\,\mathrm{cm} = 0.10\,\mathrm{m}
$$

$$
I = 5\,\mathrm{A}
$$

The point is midway between the wires, so its distance from each wire is:

$$
r = \frac{d}{2} = 0.05\,\mathrm{m}
$$

Magnetic field from one long straight wire:

$$
B = \frac{\mu_0 I}{2\pi r}
$$

$$
B = \frac{(4\pi\times10^{-7})(5)}{2\pi(0.05)}
$$

$$
B = 2.0\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}
$$

The currents are in opposite directions. At the midpoint, the magnetic fields from the two wires point in the same direction, so they add:

$$
B_{\text{total}} = 2B
$$

$$
B_{\text{total}} = 2(2.0\times10^{-5})
$$

$$
B_{\text{total}} = 4.0\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}
$$

**Answer:**

$$
\boxed{B_{\text{total}} = 4.0\times10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}}
$$

The direction is found by the right-hand rule. If the left wire carries current upward and the right wire carries current downward, the magnetic field at the midpoint is into the page.
