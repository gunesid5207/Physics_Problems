# 5. Energy Stored in a Capacitor

Given:

$$
S = 0.02\,\mathrm{m^2}
$$

$$
d = 5\,\mathrm{mm} = 0.005\,\mathrm{m}
$$

$$
V = 500\,\mathrm{V}
$$

## 1. Capacitance

For a parallel-plate capacitor:

$$
C = \varepsilon_0\frac{S}{d}
$$

$$
C = (8.85\times10^{-12})\frac{0.02}{0.005}
$$

$$
C = 3.54\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{F}
$$

$$
\boxed{C = 3.54\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{F}}
$$

## 2. Stored Energy

Use:

$$
W = \frac{1}{2}CV^2
$$

$$
W = \frac{1}{2}(3.54\times10^{-11})(500)^2
$$

$$
W = 4.43\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{J}
$$

$$
\boxed{W = 4.43\times10^{-6}\,\mathrm{J}}
$$

## 3. Electric Field Intensity

$$
E = \frac{V}{d}
$$

$$
E = \frac{500}{0.005}
$$

$$
E = 1.0\times10^5\,\mathrm{V/m}
$$

$$
\boxed{E = 1.0\times10^5\,\mathrm{V/m}}
$$

## 4. Force Between Plates

The attractive force between capacitor plates is:

$$
F = \frac{1}{2}\varepsilon_0 E^2 S
$$

$$
F = \frac{1}{2}(8.85\times10^{-12})(1.0\times10^5)^2(0.02)
$$

$$
F = 8.85\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{N}
$$

$$
\boxed{F = 8.85\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{N}}
$$
