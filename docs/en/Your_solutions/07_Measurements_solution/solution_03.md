# 3. Propagation of Error III

Given:

$$
V = (10.0 \pm 0.2)\,\mathrm{V}
$$

$$
I = (2.00 \pm 0.05)\,\mathrm{A}
$$

Using Ohm’s law:

$$
R = \frac{V}{I}
$$

Substitute the values:

$$
R = \frac{10.0}{2.00}
$$

$$
R = 5.00\,\Omega
$$

---

## Uncertainty

For division, relative uncertainties add:

$$
\frac{\Delta R}{R}
=
\frac{\Delta V}{V}
+
\frac{\Delta I}{I}
$$

$$
\frac{\Delta R}{R}
=
\frac{0.2}{10.0}
+
\frac{0.05}{2.00}
$$

$$
\frac{\Delta R}{R}
=
0.020 + 0.025
$$

$$
\frac{\Delta R}{R}
=
0.045
$$

Now calculate the absolute uncertainty:

$$
\Delta R = 0.045 \times 5.00
$$

$$
\Delta R = 0.225\,\Omega
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
R = (5.00 \pm 0.23)\,\Omega
}
$$
