# 1. Propagation of Error I

Given:

$$
r = (6.20 \pm 0.05)\,\mathrm{cm}
$$

The volume of a sphere is:

$$
V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3
$$

Substitute the radius:

$$
V = \frac{4}{3}\pi(6.20)^3
$$

$$
V \approx 998.3\,\mathrm{cm^3}
$$

---

## Uncertainty

For:

$$
V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3
$$

the relative uncertainty is:

$$
\frac{\Delta V}{V} = 3\frac{\Delta r}{r}
$$

$$
\frac{\Delta V}{V} = 3\frac{0.05}{6.20}
$$

$$
\frac{\Delta V}{V} \approx 0.0242
$$

Now calculate absolute uncertainty:

$$
\Delta V = 0.0242 \times 998.3
$$

$$
\Delta V \approx 24.2\,\mathrm{cm^3}
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{V = (998 \pm 24)\,\mathrm{cm^3}}
$$
