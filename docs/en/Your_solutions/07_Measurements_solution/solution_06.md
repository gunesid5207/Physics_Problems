# 6. Instrument Precision

Given:

$$
T = 25.4^\circ\mathrm{C}
$$

For a digital instrument,
the uncertainty is usually taken as half of the last digit.

The last digit is:

$$
0.1^\circ\mathrm{C}
$$

Therefore:

$$
\Delta T = \frac{0.1}{2}
$$

$$
\Delta T = 0.05^\circ\mathrm{C}
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
T = (25.4 \pm 0.05)^\circ\mathrm{C}
}
$$
