# 10. Light Speed Measurement

We use a microwave oven and chocolate to estimate the speed of light.

Given microwave frequency:

$$
f = 2.45\,\mathrm{GHz}
$$

$$
f = 2.45 \times 10^9\,\mathrm{Hz}
$$

Assume the measured distance between melted spots is:

$$
d = 6.1\,\mathrm{cm}
$$

Convert to meters:

$$
d = 0.061\,\mathrm{m}
$$

The distance between melted spots corresponds to half of the wavelength:

$$
\lambda = 2d
$$

$$
\lambda = 2(0.061)
$$

$$
\lambda = 0.122\,\mathrm{m}
$$

---

## Calculate the Speed of Light

Wave equation:

$$
c = f\lambda
$$

Substitute values:

$$
c =
(2.45 \times 10^9)
(0.122)
$$

$$
c \approx 2.99 \times 10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}
$$

---

## Accepted Value

Accepted speed of light:

$$
c_0 = 3.00 \times 10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}
$$

---

## Percentage Error

Formula:

$$
\%\text{ error}
=
\frac{|c-c_0|}{c_0}\times100
$$

Substitute values:

$$
\%\text{ error}
=
\frac{|2.99\times10^8 - 3.00\times10^8|}
{3.00\times10^8}
\times100
$$

$$
\%\text{ error}
\approx 0.33\%
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
c \approx 2.99\times10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\%\text{ error} \approx 0.33\%
}
$$
