# 2. Propagation of Error II

Given:

$$
L = (15.3 \pm 0.1)\,\mathrm{cm}
$$

$$
W = (8.4 \pm 0.1)\,\mathrm{cm}
$$

The area of the rectangle is:

$$
A = L \times W
$$

Substitute the values:

$$
A = 15.3 \times 8.4
$$

$$
A = 128.52\,\mathrm{cm^2}
$$

---

## Uncertainty

For multiplication, relative uncertainties add:

$$
\frac{\Delta A}{A}
=
\frac{\Delta L}{L}
+
\frac{\Delta W}{W}
$$

$$
\frac{\Delta A}{A}
=
\frac{0.1}{15.3}
+
\frac{0.1}{8.4}
$$

$$
\frac{\Delta A}{A}
=
0.00654 + 0.01190
$$

$$
\frac{\Delta A}{A}
=
0.01844
$$

Now calculate the absolute uncertainty:

$$
\Delta A = 0.01844 \times 128.52
$$

$$
\Delta A \approx 2.37\,\mathrm{cm^2}
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
A = (128.5 \pm 2.4)\,\mathrm{cm^2}
}
$$
