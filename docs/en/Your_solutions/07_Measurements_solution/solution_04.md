# 4. Relative Uncertainty

Given:

$$
v = 60\,\mathrm{km/h}
$$

The uncertainty is:

$$
5\%
$$

---

## Calculate Absolute Uncertainty

$$
\Delta v = 0.05 \times 60
$$

$$
\Delta v = 3\,\mathrm{km/h}
$$

---

## Actual Speed Range

Minimum speed:

$$
60 - 3 = 57\,\mathrm{km/h}
$$

Maximum speed:

$$
60 + 3 = 63\,\mathrm{km/h}
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
57\,\mathrm{km/h}
\le
v
\le
63\,\mathrm{km/h}
}
$$
