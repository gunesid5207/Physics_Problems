# 5. Percentage Calculation

Given:

$$
t = 5.45 \pm 0.22\ \mathrm{s}
$$

The percentage uncertainty is calculated using:

$$
\%\text{ uncertainty}
=
\frac{\Delta t}{t}\times100
$$

Substitute the values:

$$
\%\text{ uncertainty}
=
\frac{0.22}{5.45}\times100
$$

$$
\%\text{ uncertainty}
\approx 4.04\%
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
\%\text{ uncertainty} \approx 4.0\%
}
$$
