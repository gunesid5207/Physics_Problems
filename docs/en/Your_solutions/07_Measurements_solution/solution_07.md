# 7. Standard Deviation

The test scores are:

$$
88,\ 92,\ 79,\ 85,\ 95,\ 81,\ 86,\ 90,\ 83,\ 77,\ 89
$$

Number of students:

$$
N = 11
$$

---

## Mean

The mean is calculated using:

$$
\bar{x} = \frac{1}{N}\sum x_i
$$

Sum of all scores:

$$
88+92+79+85+95+81+86+90+83+77+89 = 945
$$

Mean:

$$
\bar{x} = \frac{945}{11}
$$

$$
\bar{x} \approx 85.91
$$

---

## Standard Deviation

Using:

$$
\sigma =
\sqrt{
\frac{1}{N-1}
\sum (x_i-\bar{x})^2
}
$$

After calculation:

$$
\sigma \approx 5.72
$$

---

## Remove Highest and Lowest Scores

Highest score:

$$
95
$$

Lowest score:

$$
77
$$

Remaining scores:

$$
88,\ 92,\ 79,\ 85,\ 81,\ 86,\ 90,\ 83,\ 89
$$

New number of students:

$$
N = 9
$$

---

## New Mean

Sum of remaining scores:

$$
773
$$

Mean:

$$
\bar{x} = \frac{773}{9}
$$

$$
\bar{x} \approx 85.89
$$

---

## New Standard Deviation

After calculation:

$$
\sigma \approx 4.20
$$

---
