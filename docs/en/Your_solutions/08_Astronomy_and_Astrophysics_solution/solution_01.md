# 1. Rotational Velocity

Given:

$$
R_E = 6378\,\mathrm{km}
$$

$$
T = 24\,\mathrm{h} = 86400\,\mathrm{s}
$$

The linear speed at the equator is:

$$
v = \frac{2\pi R_E}{T}
$$

Substitute the values:

$$
v = \frac{2\pi(6378)}{86400}
$$

$$
v = 0.4638\,\mathrm{km/s}
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
v \approx 0.464\,\mathrm{km/s}
}
$$

A point on Earth's equator moves at about:

$$
\boxed{
464\,\mathrm{m/s}
}
$$
