# 7. Megastructures

Given:

$$
M = 3.3 \times 10^{23}\,\mathrm{kg}
$$

Surface density:

$$
\sigma = 10\,\mathrm{kg/m^2}
$$

The surface area that can be built is:

$$
A = \frac{M}{\sigma}
$$

Substitute:

$$
A = \frac{3.3 \times 10^{23}}{10}
$$

$$
A = 3.3 \times 10^{22}\,\mathrm{m^2}
$$

For a sphere:

$$
A = 4\pi r^2
$$

Solve for \(r\):

$$
r = \sqrt{\frac{A}{4\pi}}
$$

Substitute:

$$
r =
\sqrt{
\frac{3.3 \times 10^{22}}{4\pi}
}
$$

$$
r \approx 5.12 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{m}
$$

Convert to kilometers:

$$
r \approx 5.12 \times 10^7\,\mathrm{km}
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
r \approx 5.12 \times 10^{10}\,\mathrm{m}
}
$$

or:

$$
\boxed{
r \approx 5.12 \times 10^7\,\mathrm{km}
}
$$
