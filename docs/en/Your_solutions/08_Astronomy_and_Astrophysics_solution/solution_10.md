# 10. Measuring the Height of the Atmosphere

Given:

$$
t = 40\,\mathrm{min}
$$

Earth rotates:

$$
360^\circ \text{ in } 24\,\mathrm{h}
$$

Since:

$$
24\,\mathrm{h} = 1440\,\mathrm{min}
$$

Earth rotates per minute:

$$
\frac{360^\circ}{1440}
=
0.25^\circ/\mathrm{min}
$$

---

## 1. Solar Depression Angle

For \(40\) minutes:

$$
\phi = 40 \times 0.25^\circ
$$

$$
\phi = 10^\circ
$$

---

## 2. Atmospheric Height

Given model:

$$
\cos\phi = \frac{R_E}{R_E+h}
$$

Rearrange:

$$
R_E + h = \frac{R_E}{\cos\phi}
$$

$$
h = \frac{R_E}{\cos\phi} - R_E
$$

Factor out \(R_E\):

$$
h = R_E\left(\frac{1}{\cos\phi} - 1\right)
$$

Given:

$$
R_E = 6370\,\mathrm{km}
$$

Substitute:

$$
h =
6370
\left(
\frac{1}{\cos 10^\circ}
- 1
\right)
$$

$$
h \approx 98.3\,\mathrm{km}
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
\phi = 10^\circ
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
h \approx 98\,\mathrm{km}
}
$$
