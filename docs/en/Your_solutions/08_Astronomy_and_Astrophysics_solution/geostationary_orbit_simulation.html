Geostationary Orbit

A geostationary satellite has the same angular speed as Earth, so it remains above the same point on the equator.

Required geostationary period
24 h
Altitude above Earth
35,800 km
Current drift from ground point
0.0 deg

Set the green satellite to 24 h. Its line stays locked to the marked ground point. Change the period and the satellite begins to drift east or west.
