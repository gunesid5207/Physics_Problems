# 5. Escape Velocity

Given:

$$
M_M = 7.35 \times 10^{22}\,\mathrm{kg}
$$

$$
R_M = 1737\,\mathrm{km}
$$

Convert the Moon's radius to meters:

$$
R_M = 1.737 \times 10^6\,\mathrm{m}
$$

The escape velocity is:

$$
v_{\mathrm{esc}} =
\sqrt{\frac{2GM_M}{R_M}}
$$

Substitute:

$$
v_{\mathrm{esc}} =
\sqrt{
\frac{2(6.67 \times 10^{-11})(7.35 \times 10^{22})}
{1.737 \times 10^6}
}
$$

$$
v_{\mathrm{esc}} \approx 2.38 \times 10^3\,\mathrm{m/s}
$$

Convert to km/s:

$$
v_{\mathrm{esc}} \approx 2.38\,\mathrm{km/s}
$$

---

## Fraction of Earth's Escape Velocity

Earth's escape velocity is approximately:

$$
v_{\mathrm{esc,E}} = 11.2\,\mathrm{km/s}
$$

So:

$$
\frac{v_{\mathrm{esc,M}}}{v_{\mathrm{esc,E}}}
=
\frac{2.38}{11.2}
$$

$$
\frac{v_{\mathrm{esc,M}}}{v_{\mathrm{esc,E}}}
\approx 0.212
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
v_{\mathrm{esc,Moon}} \approx 2.38\,\mathrm{km/s}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
v_{\mathrm{esc,Moon}} \approx 0.21\,v_{\mathrm{esc,Earth}}
}
$$
