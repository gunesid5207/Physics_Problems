# 8. Interplanetary Travel

Given distance:

$$
d = 55\,\mathrm{million\,km}
$$

$$
d = 55 \times 10^6\,\mathrm{km}
$$

---

## a) Message Sent at the Speed of Light

Speed of light:

$$
c = 299792\,\mathrm{km/s}
$$

Time:

$$
t = \frac{d}{c}
$$

Substitute:

$$
t =
\frac{55 \times 10^6}{299792}
$$

$$
t \approx 183\,\mathrm{s}
$$

Convert to minutes:

$$
t = \frac{183}{60}
$$

$$
t \approx 3.06\,\mathrm{min}
$$

---

## b) Spacecraft at 40000 km/h

Speed:

$$
v = 40000\,\mathrm{km/h}
$$

Time:

$$
t = \frac{d}{v}
$$

Substitute:

$$
t =
\frac{55 \times 10^6}{40000}
$$

$$
t = 1375\,\mathrm{h}
$$

Convert to days:

$$
t = \frac{1375}{24}
$$

$$
t \approx 57.3\,\mathrm{days}
$$

---

## c) Airplane at 900 km/h

Speed:

$$
v = 900\,\mathrm{km/h}
$$

Time:

$$
t = \frac{d}{v}
$$

Substitute:

$$
t =
\frac{55 \times 10^6}{900}
$$

$$
t \approx 61111\,\mathrm{h}
$$

Convert to days:

$$
t =
\frac{61111}{24}
$$

$$
t \approx 2546\,\mathrm{days}
$$

Convert to years:

$$
t =
\frac{2546}{365}
$$

$$
t \approx 6.98\,\mathrm{years}
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
t_{\mathrm{light}} \approx 183\,\mathrm{s} \approx 3.06\,\mathrm{min}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
t_{\mathrm{spacecraft}} \approx 1375\,\mathrm{h} \approx 57.3\,\mathrm{days}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
t_{\mathrm{airplane}} \approx 61111\,\mathrm{h} \approx 2546\,\mathrm{days} \approx 7.0\,\mathrm{years}
}
$$
