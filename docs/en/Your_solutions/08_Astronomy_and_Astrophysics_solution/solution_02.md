# 2. Orbital Mechanics

## ISS Orbital Speed

Given:

$$
h = 400\,\mathrm{km}
$$

$$
R_E = 6378\,\mathrm{km}
$$

$$
M_E = 5.97 \times 10^{24}\,\mathrm{kg}
$$

$$
G = 6.67 \times 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\,m^2/kg^2}
$$

The orbital radius of the ISS is:

$$
r = R_E + h
$$

$$
r = 6378 + 400
$$

$$
r = 6778\,\mathrm{km}
$$

Convert to meters:

$$
r = 6.778 \times 10^6\,\mathrm{m}
$$

For a circular orbit:

$$
v = \sqrt{\frac{GM_E}{r}}
$$

Substitute:

$$
v =
\sqrt{
\frac{(6.67 \times 10^{-11})(5.97 \times 10^{24})}
{6.778 \times 10^6}
}
$$

$$
v \approx 7.67 \times 10^3\,\mathrm{m/s}
$$

$$
v \approx 7.67\,\mathrm{km/s}
$$

---

## Earth's Orbital Speed Around the Sun

Given:

$$
r = 150 \times 10^6\,\mathrm{km}
$$

$$
T = 365.25\,\mathrm{days}
$$

Convert the period to seconds:

$$
T = 365.25 \times 24 \times 3600
$$

$$
T = 3.156 \times 10^7\,\mathrm{s}
$$

Use:

$$
v = \frac{2\pi r}{T}
$$

Substitute:

$$
v =
\frac{2\pi(150 \times 10^6)}
{3.156 \times 10^7}
$$

$$
v \approx 29.9\,\mathrm{km/s}
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
v_{\mathrm{ISS}} \approx 7.67\,\mathrm{km/s}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
v_{\mathrm{Earth}} \approx 29.9\,\mathrm{km/s}
}
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{
\text{Earth moves faster around the Sun than the ISS moves around Earth.}
}
$$
