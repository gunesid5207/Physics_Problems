# 6. Solar Gravity

Given:

$$
M_S = 2.0 \times 10^{30}\,\mathrm{kg}
$$

$$
R_S = 6.96 \times 10^8\,\mathrm{m}
$$

The gravitational acceleration at the surface of the Sun is:

$$
g_S = \frac{GM_S}{R_S^2}
$$

Substitute:

$$
g_S =
\frac{(6.67 \times 10^{-11})(2.0 \times 10^{30})}
{(6.96 \times 10^8)^2}
$$

$$
g_S \approx 2.76 \times 10^2\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
$$

$$
g_S \approx 276\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
$$

---

## Weight Increase Factor

On Earth:

$$
g_E \approx 9.81\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
$$

The factor is:

$$
\frac{g_S}{g_E}
=
\frac{276}{9.81}
$$

$$
\frac{g_S}{g_E}
\approx 28.1
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
g_S \approx 276\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
28.1\text{ times}
}
