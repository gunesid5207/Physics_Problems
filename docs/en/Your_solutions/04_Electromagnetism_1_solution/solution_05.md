## 5. Field Levitation

**Given**

- Proton mass:

$$
m_p = 1.67 \times 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}
$$

- Proton charge:

$$
e = 1.60 \times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}
$$

- Gravitational acceleration:

$$
g = 9.8\,\mathrm{m/s^2}
$$

**Analysis**

For levitation, the electric force must balance the weight:

$$
qE = mg
$$

So,

$$
E = \frac{mg}{q}
$$

Substitute the values:

$$
E = \frac{(1.67 \times 10^{-27})(9.8)}{1.60 \times 10^{-19}}
$$

$$
E \approx 1.02 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{N/C}
$$

**Result**

$$
\boxed{E \approx 1.02 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{N/C}}
$$

**Direction**

The electric field must point **upward**, so that the electric force on the positive proton balances gravity.

**Check**

Electric force $qE$ must equal $mg$, so the order of magnitude ($10^{-7}$) is consistent.
