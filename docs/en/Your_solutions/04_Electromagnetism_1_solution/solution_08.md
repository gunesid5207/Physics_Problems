## 8. Lorentz Force

**Given**

- Charge:

$$
q = 2 \times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}
$$

- Mass:

$$
m = 4 \times 10^{-27}\,\mathrm{kg}
$$

- Magnetic field:

$$
B = 0.5\,\mathrm{T}
$$

- Speed:

$$
v = 1.0 \times 10^6\,\mathrm{m/s}
$$

The particle enters the field perpendicular to $\vec B$.

**Analysis**

The magnetic Lorentz force is

$$
F = qvB\sin\theta
$$

Since the velocity is perpendicular to the magnetic field,

$$
\theta = 90^\circ \quad \Rightarrow \quad \sin\theta = 1
$$

So,

$$
F = qvB
$$

Substitute the values:

$$
F = (2 \times 10^{-19})(1.0 \times 10^6)(0.5)
$$

$$
F = 1.0 \times 10^{-13}\,\mathrm{N}
$$

**Result**

$$
\boxed{F = 1.0 \times 10^{-13}\,\mathrm{N}}
$$

**Check**

The mass is not needed here.  
If mass appears in your final formula, the setup is wrong.
