## 7. Cyclotron Motion

**Given**

- Accelerating potential difference:

$$
V = 5000\,\mathrm{V}
$$

- Magnetic field:

$$
B = 0.1\,\mathrm{T}
$$

- Electron charge:

$$
e = 1.60 \times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}
$$

- Electron mass:

$$
m_e = 9.11 \times 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}
$$

**Analysis**

The electron is accelerated from rest through a potential difference $V$, so its kinetic energy is

$$
eV = \frac{1}{2}mv^2
$$

Hence,

$$
v = \sqrt{\frac{2eV}{m}}
$$

Substitute the values:

$$
v = \sqrt{\frac{2(1.60 \times 10^{-19})(5000)}{9.11 \times 10^{-31}}}
$$

$$
v \approx 4.19 \times 10^7\,\mathrm{m/s}
$$

In a magnetic field perpendicular to the velocity, the magnetic force provides the centripetal force:

$$
evB = \frac{mv^2}{r}
$$

So,

$$
r = \frac{mv}{eB}
$$

Substitute the values:

$$
r = \frac{(9.11 \times 10^{-31})(4.19 \times 10^7)}{(1.60 \times 10^{-19})(0.1)}
$$

$$
r \approx 2.38 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}
$$

**Result**

$$
\boxed{r \approx 2.38 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}}
$$

or

$$
\boxed{r \approx 2.38\,\mathrm{mm}}
$$

**Check**

The radius is small because the electron mass is very small.
