# Problem 6: Wave Equation

The wave is given by

$$
y(x,t) = 0.05 \sin(2\pi x - 50\pi t)
$$

We compare it with the standard form of a traveling wave:

$$
y(x,t) = A \sin(kx - \omega t)
$$

Thus, we identify:

- $A = 0.05\ \text{m}$
- $k = 2\pi\ \text{rad/m}$
- $\omega = 50\pi\ \text{rad/s}$

## a) Amplitude

The amplitude is the coefficient in front of the sine function:

$$
\boxed{A = 0.05\ \text{m}}
$$

## b) Wavelength

The wave number is related to wavelength by

$$
k = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}
$$

So,

$$
\lambda = \frac{2\pi}{k} = \frac{2\pi}{2\pi} = 1\ \text{m}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{\lambda = 1\ \text{m}}
$$

## c) Frequency

Angular frequency and frequency are related by

$$
\omega = 2\pi f
$$

So,

$$
f = \frac{\omega}{2\pi} = \frac{50\pi}{2\pi} = 25\ \text{Hz}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{f = 25\ \text{Hz}}
$$

## d) Wave speed

The wave speed is

$$
v = \frac{\omega}{k}
$$

So,

$$
v = \frac{50\pi}{2\pi} = 25\ \text{m/s}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\boxed{v = 25\ \text{m/s}}
$$

## Final Answer

- Amplitude: $\boxed{0.05\ \text{m}}$
- Wavelength: $\boxed{1\ \text{m}}$
- Frequency: $\boxed{25\ \text{Hz}}$
- Wave speed: $\boxed{25\ \text{m/s}}$
