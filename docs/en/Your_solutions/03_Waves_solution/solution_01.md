# Problem 1: Wave Properties

The wavelength of a wave is given by

$$
\lambda = \frac{v}{f}
$$

where $v$ is the wave speed and $f$ is the frequency.

Given:

- $f = 440\ \text{Hz}$

## Wavelength in air

For air:

$$
v_{\text{air}} = 343\ \text{m/s}
$$

So,

$$
\lambda_{\text{air}} = \frac{343}{440} \approx 0.7795\ \text{m}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\lambda_{\text{air}} \approx 0.78\ \text{m}
$$

## Wavelength in water

For water:

$$
v_{\text{water}} = 1482\ \text{m/s}
$$

So,

$$
\lambda_{\text{water}} = \frac{1482}{440} \approx 3.3682\ \text{m}
$$

Therefore,

$$
\lambda_{\text{water}} \approx 3.37\ \text{m}
$$

## Final Answer

- Wavelength in air: $0.78\ \text{m}$
- Wavelength in water: $3.37\ \text{m}$

The wavelength is greater in water because the wave speed is greater while the frequency remains constant.
