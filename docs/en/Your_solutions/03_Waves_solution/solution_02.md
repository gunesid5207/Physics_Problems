# Problem 2: String Harmonics

For a string fixed at both ends, the fundamental mode has one antinode and corresponds to half of a wavelength along the string. Therefore,

$$
L = \frac{\lambda}{2}
$$

so

$$
\lambda = 2L
$$

Given:

- $L = 64\ \text{cm} = 0.64\ \text{m}$
- $f_1 = 330\ \text{Hz}$

Then the wavelength of the fundamental mode is

$$
\lambda = 2(0.64) = 1.28\ \text{m}
$$

The wave speed is

$$
v = f\lambda
$$

so

$$
v = 330 \times 1.28 = 422.4\ \text{m/s}
$$

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{v = 422.4\ \text{m/s}}
$$
