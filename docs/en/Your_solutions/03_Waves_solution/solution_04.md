# Problem 4: Phase Difference

The phase difference between two points separated by a distance $\Delta x$ is

$$
\Delta \phi = k \Delta x
$$

where

$$
k = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda}
$$

Given:

$$
\Delta x = \frac{\lambda}{3}
$$

Substituting into the formula:

$$
\Delta \phi = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda} \cdot \frac{\lambda}{3}
$$

$$
\Delta \phi = \frac{2\pi}{3}
$$

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{\Delta \phi = \frac{2\pi}{3}\ \text{rad}}
$$
