# Problem 5: Echo Ranging

When a person hears an echo, the sound has traveled to the obstacle and back. Therefore, the total distance traveled is twice the distance to the cliff.

The relation between distance, speed, and time is

$$
d_{\text{total}} = v t
$$

Given:

- $v = 343\ \text{m/s}$
- $t = 1\ \text{s}$

So,

$$
d_{\text{total}} = 343 \times 1 = 343\ \text{m}
$$

Since this is the round-trip distance, the distance to the cliff is

$$
d = \frac{343}{2} = 171.5\ \text{m}
$$

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{d = 171.5\ \text{m}}
$$
