# Problem 9: Momentum Comparison

Momentum is defined by

$$
p = mv
$$

where \(m\) is mass and \(v\) is velocity.

---

## 1. Fly

Mass of the fly:

$$
2\ \text{g} = 0.002\ \text{kg}
$$

Velocity:

$$
v = 10\ \text{m/s}
$$

Momentum:

$$
p_{\text{fly}} = 0.002 \cdot 10 = 0.02\ \text{kg·m/s}
$$

---

## 2. Tennis ball

Mass of the tennis ball:

$$
60\ \text{g} = 0.06\ \text{kg}
$$

Velocity:

$$
v = 1\ \text{m/s}
$$

Momentum:

$$
p_{\text{ball}} = 0.06 \cdot 1 = 0.06\ \text{kg·m/s}
$$

---

## 3. Comparison

We compare:

$$
0.06 > 0.02
$$

So the tennis ball has greater momentum.

---

## Final answer

The tennis ball has greater momentum because

$$
p_{\text{ball}} = 0.06\ \text{kg·m/s}
$$

while

$$
p_{\text{fly}} = 0.02\ \text{kg·m/s}
$$
