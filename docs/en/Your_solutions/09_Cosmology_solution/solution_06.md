# 6. Galactic Communication

Assume the two civilizations are located at opposite ends of the Milky Way.

The approximate diameter of the Milky Way is:

$$
d \approx 100000\,\mathrm{ly}
$$

A message traveling at the speed of light takes one year to travel one light-year.

So, one message takes:

$$
t_{\mathrm{one-way}} = 100000\,\mathrm{years}
$$

For mutual greetings, one civilization sends a greeting and then receives a reply.

Therefore, the round-trip time is:

$$
t_{\mathrm{round-trip}} = 2(100000)
$$

$$
t_{\mathrm{round-trip}} = 200000\,\mathrm{years}
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
t_{\mathrm{one-way}} \approx 100000\,\mathrm{years}
}
$$

For an exchange of greetings:

$$
\boxed{
t_{\mathrm{round-trip}} \approx 200000\,\mathrm{years}
}
$$
