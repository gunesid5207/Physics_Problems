# 10. Cosmic Microwave Background

Given Wien's displacement law:

$$
\lambda_{\mathrm{peak}} = \frac{b}{T}
$$

where:

$$
b = 2.898 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m\,K}
$$

---

## At Recombination

Given:

$$
T = 3000\,\mathrm{K}
$$

Substitute:

$$
\lambda_{\mathrm{peak}}
=
\frac{2.898 \times 10^{-3}}{3000}
$$

$$
\lambda_{\mathrm{peak}}
=
9.66 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}
$$

Convert to micrometers:

$$
9.66 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}
=
0.966\,\mu\mathrm{m}
$$

---

## Present CMB

Given:

$$
T = 2.7\,\mathrm{K}
$$

Substitute:

$$
\lambda_{\mathrm{peak}}
=
\frac{2.898 \times 10^{-3}}{2.7}
$$

$$
\lambda_{\mathrm{peak}}
\approx 1.07 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}
$$

Convert to millimeters:

$$
1.07 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}
=
1.07\,\mathrm{mm}
$$

---

## Final Answer

At recombination:

$$
\boxed{
\lambda_{\mathrm{peak}} \approx 9.66 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}
\approx 0.966\,\mu\mathrm{m}
}
$$

Now:

$$
\boxed{
\lambda_{\mathrm{peak}} \approx 1.07 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m}
\approx 1.07\,\mathrm{mm}
}
$$
