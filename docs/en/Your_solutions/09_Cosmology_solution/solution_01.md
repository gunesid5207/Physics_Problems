# 1. Cosmic Chronology

The events must be placed from earliest to latest.

Given events:

- Recombination
- Big Bang Nucleosynthesis
- Inflation
- Formation of first galaxies
- Quark-gluon plasma era

---

## Order

The earliest event is inflation, which happened extremely soon after the Big Bang.

After inflation, the universe was still extremely hot and dense, and quarks and gluons existed in a quark-gluon plasma.

Later, protons and neutrons formed, and Big Bang nucleosynthesis produced the first light nuclei.

Much later, electrons combined with nuclei to form neutral atoms. This is called recombination.

Finally, after matter collected under gravity, the first galaxies formed.

---

\rightarrow
\text{Formation of first galaxies}
}
$$
