# 7. Galactic Trivia

## a) Ratio of Diameter to Thickness

Take the approximate diameter of the Milky Way as:

$$
D \approx 100000\,\mathrm{ly}
$$

Take the approximate thickness as:

$$
h \approx 1000\,\mathrm{ly}
$$

The ratio is:

$$
\frac{D}{h}
=
\frac{100000}{1000}
$$

$$
\frac{D}{h} = 100
$$

---

## b) Stars per Person

Given:

$$
N_{\mathrm{stars}} = 200\,\mathrm{billion}
$$

Assume Earth's population is about:

$$
N_{\mathrm{people}} = 8\,\mathrm{billion}
$$

Then:

$$
\frac{N_{\mathrm{stars}}}{N_{\mathrm{people}}}
=
\frac{200\,\mathrm{billion}}{8\,\mathrm{billion}}
$$

$$
= 25
$$

---

## c) Time to Count the Stars

If one star is counted each second:

$$
t = 200 \times 10^9\,\mathrm{s}
$$

Convert seconds to years:

$$
t =
\frac{200 \times 10^9}{365.25 \times 24 \times 3600}
$$

$$
t \approx 6338\,\mathrm{years}
$$

---
