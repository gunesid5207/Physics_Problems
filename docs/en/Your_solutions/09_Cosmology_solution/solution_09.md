# 9. Doppler Effect

Given:

$$
v = 5 \times 10^6\,\mathrm{m/s}
$$

$$
c = 3.00 \times 10^8\,\mathrm{m/s}
$$

$$
\lambda_{\mathrm{emitted}} = 486\,\mathrm{nm}
$$

First calculate:

$$
\frac{v}{c}
=
\frac{5 \times 10^6}{3.00 \times 10^8}
$$

$$
\frac{v}{c}
\approx 0.0167
$$

---

## Non-Relativistic Doppler Shift

Formula:

$$
\lambda_{\mathrm{observed}}
=
\lambda_{\mathrm{emitted}}
\left(1+\frac{v}{c}\right)
$$

Substitute:

$$
\lambda_{\mathrm{observed}}
=
486(1+0.0167)
$$

$$
\lambda_{\mathrm{observed}}
\approx 494.1\,\mathrm{nm}
$$

---

## Relativistic Doppler Shift

Formula:

$$
\lambda_{\mathrm{observed}}
=
\lambda_{\mathrm{emitted}}
\sqrt{
\frac{1+\frac{v}{c}}
{1-\frac{v}{c}}
}
$$

Substitute:

$$
\lambda_{\mathrm{observed}}
=
486
\sqrt{
\frac{1+0.0167}
{1-0.0167}
}
$$

$$
\lambda_{\mathrm{observed}}
\approx 494.2\,\mathrm{nm}
$$

---

## Comparison

The difference is:

$$
494.2 - 494.1 \approx 0.07\,\mathrm{nm}
$$

The relativistic result is slightly larger.

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
\lambda_{\mathrm{non-rel}} \approx 494.1\,\mathrm{nm}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\lambda_{\mathrm{rel}} \approx 494.2\,\mathrm{nm}
}
$$

The two results are very close because:

$$
\boxed{
v \ll c
}
$$
