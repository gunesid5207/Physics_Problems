# 3. Cosmic Sizes

We order the objects from smallest to largest.

Approximate sizes:

$$
\text{Human} \sim 1.7\,\mathrm{m}
$$

$$
\text{Ceres} \sim 940\,\mathrm{km}
$$

$$
\text{Pluto} \sim 2376\,\mathrm{km}
$$

$$
\text{Moon} \sim 3474\,\mathrm{km}
$$

$$
\text{Earth} \sim 12742\,\mathrm{km}
$$

$$
\text{Jupiter} \sim 143000\,\mathrm{km}
$$

$$
\text{Sun} \sim 1.39 \times 10^6\,\mathrm{km}
$$

The Solar System is much larger than the Sun, and galaxies are far larger than planetary systems.

---

## Final Answer

From smallest to largest:

$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\text{Human} \\
&\rightarrow \text{Ceres} \\
&\rightarrow \text{Pluto} \\
&\rightarrow \text{Moon} \\
&\rightarrow \text{Earth} \\
&\rightarrow \text{Jupiter} \\
&\rightarrow \text{Sun} \\
&\rightarrow \text{Solar System} \\
&\rightarrow \text{Milky Way galaxy} \\
&\rightarrow \text{Andromeda galaxy} \\
&\rightarrow \text{Local Group of galaxies} \\
&\rightarrow \text{Observable universe}
\end{aligned}
}
$$
