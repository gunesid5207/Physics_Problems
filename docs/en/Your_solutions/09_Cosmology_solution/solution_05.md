# 5. Galactic Collision

Given:

$$
d = 2.5 \times 10^6\,\mathrm{ly}
$$

$$
v = 110\,\mathrm{km/s}
$$

Convert distance to kilometers:

$$
1\,\mathrm{ly} \approx 9.46 \times 10^{12}\,\mathrm{km}
$$

$$
d =
(2.5 \times 10^6)(9.46 \times 10^{12})
$$

$$
d \approx 2.37 \times 10^{19}\,\mathrm{km}
$$

Time:

$$
t = \frac{d}{v}
$$

Substitute:

$$
t =
\frac{2.37 \times 10^{19}}{110}
$$

$$
t \approx 2.15 \times 10^{17}\,\mathrm{s}
$$

Convert to years:

$$
t =
\frac{2.15 \times 10^{17}}{3.156 \times 10^7}
$$

$$
t \approx 6.8 \times 10^9\,\mathrm{years}
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
t \approx 6.8 \times 10^9\,\mathrm{years}
}
$$

So the estimated collision time is about:

$$
\boxed{
6.8\,\mathrm{billion\,years}
}
$$
