# Problem 2: Harmonic Motion

A mass of

$$
m = 10\ \text{kg}
$$

oscillates according to

$$
x(t)=0.2\cos(10\pi t)
$$

where \(x\) is in meters.

We want to find:

1. The spring constant \(k\)
2. The total mechanical energy of the system

---

## 1. Identify the angular frequency

For simple harmonic motion, the standard form is

$$
x(t)=A\cos(\omega t)
$$

Comparing with

$$
x(t)=0.2\cos(10\pi t)
$$

we get:

$$
A=0.2\ \text{m}
$$

$$
\omega = 10\pi\ \text{rad/s}
$$

---

## 2. Find the spring constant

For a mass-spring system,

$$
\omega = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}
$$

So,

$$
k = m\omega^2
$$

Substitute the values:

$$
k = 10(10\pi)^2
$$

$$
k = 10 \cdot 100\pi^2
$$

$$
k = 1000\pi^2\ \text{N/m}
$$

Numerically,

$$
k \approx 1000(9.87) \approx 9869.6\ \text{N/m}
$$

So,

$$
\boxed{k \approx 9.87\times 10^3\ \text{N/m}}
$$

---

## 3. Total mechanical energy

The total mechanical energy of a spring-mass system is

$$
E = \frac{1}{2}kA^2
$$

Substitute the values:

$$
E = \frac{1}{2}(1000\pi^2)(0.2)^2
$$

Since

$$
(0.2)^2 = 0.04
$$

we get

$$
E = \frac{1}{2}(1000\pi^2)(0.04)
$$

$$
E = 20\pi^2
$$

Numerically,

$$
E \approx 20(9.87) \approx 197.4\ \text{J}
$$

So,

$$
\boxed{E \approx 197.4\ \text{J}}
$$

---

## Final answers

The spring constant is

$$
\boxed{k = 1000\pi^2\ \text{N/m} \approx 9869.6\ \text{N/m}}
$$

The total mechanical energy is

$$
\boxed{E = 20\pi^2\ \text{J} \approx 197.4\ \text{J}}
$$
