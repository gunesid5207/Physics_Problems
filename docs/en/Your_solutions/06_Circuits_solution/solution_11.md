# 11. Power & Energy

Given:

$$
R = 100\,\Omega
$$

$$
V = 50\,\mathrm{V}
$$

Time:

$$
t = 5\,\text{minutes}
$$

Convert time to seconds:

$$
t = 5 \times 60
$$

$$
t = 300\,\mathrm{s}
$$

---

## Step 1: Power

Use:

$$
P = \frac{V^2}{R}
$$

Substitute values:

$$
P = \frac{50^2}{100}
$$

$$
P = \frac{2500}{100}
$$

$$
P = 25\,\mathrm{W}
$$

---

## Step 2: Energy

Use:

$$
E = Pt
$$

$$
E = 25 \times 300
$$

$$
E = 7500\,\mathrm{J}
$$

---

## Final Answers

Power:

$$
\boxed{P = 25\,\mathrm{W}}
$$

Energy consumed:

$$
\boxed{E = 7500\,\mathrm{J}}
$$
