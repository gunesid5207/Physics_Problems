# 7. Capacitors in Parallel

Given:

$$
C_1 = 4\,\mu\mathrm{F}
$$

$$
C_2 = 6\,\mu\mathrm{F}
$$

$$
V = 10\,\mathrm{V}
$$

---

## Step 1: Total Capacitance

For capacitors in parallel:

$$
C_{\text{eq}} = C_1 + C_2
$$

$$
C_{\text{eq}} = 4 + 6
$$

$$
C_{\text{eq}} = 10\,\mu\mathrm{F}
$$

Convert to SI units:

$$
C_{\text{eq}} = 10 \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{F}
$$

---

## Step 2: Total Charge

Use:

$$
Q = CV
$$

$$
Q = (10\times10^{-6})(10)
$$

$$
Q = 1.0\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{C}
$$

---

## Step 3: Total Energy

Use:

$$
U = \frac{1}{2}CV^2
$$

$$
U = \frac{1}{2}(10\times10^{-6})(10)^2
$$

$$
U = 5.0\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{J}
$$

---

## Final Answers

Total charge:

$$
\boxed{Q = 1.0\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{C}}
$$

Total energy:

$$
\boxed{U = 5.0\times10^{-4}\,\mathrm{J}}
$$
