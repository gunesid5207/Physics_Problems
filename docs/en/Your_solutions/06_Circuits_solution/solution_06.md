# 6. Kirchhoff's Laws Again

Given:

$$
\mathcal{E}_1 = 9\,\mathrm{V}
$$

$$
\mathcal{E}_2 = 4.5\,\mathrm{V}
$$

$$
R_1 = 10\,\Omega
$$

$$
R_2 = 20\,\Omega
$$

Internal resistances:

$$
r_w = 1\,\Omega
$$

---

The circuit forms a single loop.

Total resistance:

$$
R_{\text{total}} = 10 + 20 + 1 + 1
$$

$$
R_{\text{total}} = 32\,\Omega
$$

---

The batteries oppose each other, so the effective voltage is:

$$
\mathcal{E}_{\text{net}} = 9 - 4.5
$$

$$
\mathcal{E}_{\text{net}} = 4.5\,\mathrm{V}
$$

---

Using Ohm's law:

$$
I = \frac{\mathcal{E}_{\text{net}}}{R_{\text{total}}}
$$

$$
I = \frac{4.5}{32}
$$

$$
I \approx 0.141\,\mathrm{A}
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{I \approx 0.141\,\mathrm{A}}
$$

The ammeter reads approximately:

$$
\boxed{0.14\,\mathrm{A}}
$$
