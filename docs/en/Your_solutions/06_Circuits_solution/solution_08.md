# 8. AC Voltage Equation

Given current:

$$
I(t) = 2\sin(120\pi t)
$$

Resistance:

$$
R = 50\,\Omega
$$

---

Using Ohm's law:

$$
V(t) = IR
$$

Substitute the current function:

$$
V(t) = 50 \times 2\sin(120\pi t)
$$

$$
V(t) = 100\sin(120\pi t)
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{
V(t) = 100\sin(120\pi t)\,\mathrm{V}
}
$$
