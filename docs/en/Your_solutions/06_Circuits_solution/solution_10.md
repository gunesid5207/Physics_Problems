# 10. Average Current

Given:

$$
Q = 30\,\mathrm{C}
$$

Time:

$$
t = 2\,\mathrm{ms}
$$

Convert milliseconds to seconds:

$$
t = 2\times10^{-3}\,\mathrm{s}
$$

---

Average current formula:

$$
I = \frac{Q}{t}
$$

Substitute values:

$$
I = \frac{30}{2\times10^{-3}}
$$

$$
I = 1.5\times10^4\,\mathrm{A}
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{I = 1.5\times10^4\,\mathrm{A}}
$$
