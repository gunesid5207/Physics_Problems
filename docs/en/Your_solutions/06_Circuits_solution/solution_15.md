# 15. Resistor Cube

A cube is made from 12 identical resistors.

Each edge has resistance:

$$
R
$$

We need the equivalent resistance between two opposite corners of the cube.

---

By symmetry, the current splits equally into the three edges leaving the first corner.

The equivalent resistance between opposite corners of a resistor cube is a known result:

$$
R_{\text{eq}} = \frac{5}{6}R
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{R_{\text{eq}} = \frac{5}{6}R}
$$
