# 12. Transformer Currents

Given:

$$
N_p = 1000
$$

$$
N_s = 200
$$

$$
V_p = 120\,\mathrm{V}
$$

$$
I_s = 3\,\mathrm{A}
$$

---

## Step 1: Secondary Voltage

Transformer relation:

$$
\frac{V_s}{V_p} = \frac{N_s}{N_p}
$$

Substitute values:

$$
\frac{V_s}{120} = \frac{200}{1000}
$$

$$
\frac{V_s}{120} = 0.2
$$

$$
V_s = 24\,\mathrm{V}
$$

---

## Step 2: Primary Current

For an ideal transformer:

$$
V_p I_p = V_s I_s
$$

Solve for primary current:

$$
I_p = \frac{V_s I_s}{V_p}
$$

$$
I_p = \frac{24 \times 3}{120}
$$

$$
I_p = 0.6\,\mathrm{A}
$$

---

## Final Answers

Secondary voltage:

$$
\boxed{V_s = 24\,\mathrm{V}}
$$

Primary current:

$$
\boxed{I_p = 0.6\,\mathrm{A}}
$$
