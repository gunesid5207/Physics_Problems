# 13. Transformer Ratio

Given:

$$
V_p = 120\,\mathrm{V}
$$

$$
V_s = 9.0\,\mathrm{V}
$$

$$
N_p = 400
$$

We use the transformer equation:

$$
\frac{V_s}{V_p} = \frac{N_s}{N_p}
$$

Substitute values:

$$
\frac{9}{120} = \frac{N_s}{400}
$$

Solve for $N_s$:

$$
N_s = 400 \times \frac{9}{120}
$$

$$
N_s = 30
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{N_s = 30}
$$

The secondary coil must have:

$$
\boxed{30\ \text{turns}}
$$
